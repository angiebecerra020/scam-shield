Q&A

Scam call protection: your questions, answered.

The things families ask us most, in plain words.

What is Scam Shield?

Scam Shield listens for scam tricks on your loved one's phone calls, with their OK. If a call looks like a scam, it calls you right away so you can step in while there's still time.

Does my loved one have to agree?

Yes. When you sign someone up, we call them on their own phone and they press 1 to allow it or 2 to say no. Nothing is listened to until they say YES.

How do they turn it off?

They call the Scam Shield number from their own phone and press 2. You can also pause or remove them from your dashboard.

Do you record the calls?

No. We never record or save the sound of a call. We read the words while the call is going to check for scams, and then let them go.

What happens when a call looks like a scam?

NVIDIA Nemotron scores each call from 0 to 100. When it reaches 70, we call you in your language, tell you why we're worried, and you can press 1 to join the call yourself.

Do they keep their phone number?

Yes. Their calls forward to Scam Shield, which rings an app on their cell phone or their own home phone through a small adapter.

Does it work with a landline?

Yes. A landline needs a small phone adapter, about $40. A cell phone needs nothing extra.

What languages does it speak?

English and Spanish. Use the switch at the top of any page.

Can it be wrong?

Yes. AI can miss a scam or warn about a call that's fine. Always call your family member back on a number you already know. Scam Shield isn't for emergencies: if someone is in danger, call 911.

How can I try it?

Watch the live demo, or use Test it to call our number and play the scammer yourself. No account needed.

Does it work anywhere in the US?

Yes. It works with US phone numbers in every state, on a cell phone or a home landline.
