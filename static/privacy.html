Privacy

Your calls are private. Here's exactly what we keep.

Scam Shield is a hackathon project by Angie and Cass for the Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon 2026. We wrote this in plain English so anyone in the family can read it.

Last updated October 28, 2026

We only listen with permission

Scam Shield only listens to a senior's calls after they say YES on their own phone. Florida, where we're based, requires everyone on a call to agree before it's recorded, so we never record or transcribe anyone without their OK. Our demos use acted, made up calls.

What we never keep

  • No audio. We never record or save the sound of a call.
  • No full transcripts. We read the words while the call is going to check for scams, and then let them go.
  • No ads and no tracking. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't use tracking cookies or analytics.

What we keep

  • Family accounts: your email and login, so only your family sees your people.
  • The people you protect: their name and phone number, your name and phone number, the languages you each read, and their YES or NO answer.
  • Call history: the caller's number, the time, the highest scam score, the reasons, who the caller said they were, the warning links we found, and your "Was this a scam?" answer.
  • Your scam list: numbers you reported, and whether to warn you or block them.

The "Call it yourself" test line

  • We turn your words into text while you talk, and keep that text in memory for up to 2 hours so you can see it on the page. It never goes in our database.
  • We don't save your phone number. We keep a scrambled version for one day, only to limit how many test calls a phone can make.
  • If you type an email, we only use it to send you that one alert.

Who helps us run Scam Shield

  • NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory reads the words of a call to score how risky it is.
  • Tavily searches the web for matching scam warnings and reports about a caller's number.
  • Twilio carries the phone calls and turns speech into text.
  • Supabase keeps family logins, Gmail sends alert emails, and Render hosts the site.

What your browser remembers

Your browser remembers your language choice and, if you log in, that you're logged in. That's it. Nothing is used to track you.

Deleting your information

You can pause protection anytime from the dashboard. To delete everything about you or someone you protect, email us at scam.shield.demo@gmail.com and we'll take care of it. Since this is a hackathon demo, we'll delete the demo's data after judging ends.

Kids

Scam Shield is for adults and their families. It's not meant for children under 13.
