How it works

  1. They give consent

    We call your loved one first. Nothing is monitored until they agree.

  2. We monitor for scams

    NVIDIA Nemotron scores each call from 0 to 100 and identifies tactics such as gift card requests, secrecy and pressure to act quickly.

  3. You receive an alert

    We call you immediately and explain why the call appears risky. Press 1 to join the call.
