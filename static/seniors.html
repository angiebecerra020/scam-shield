Scam call protection for seniors

Scam call protection for seniors, while the call is happening.

Scammers call older adults all over the United States pretending to be a grandchild, the bank, Medicare or the IRS. Scam Shield listens for their tricks on your loved one's calls, with their OK, and warns you before the money is gone.

The scam tricks we listen for

NVIDIA Nemotron reads the words of each call while it's going and scores it from 0 to 100. These are the tricks that push the score up:

  • Strange ways to pay: gift cards, cryptocurrency or a Bitcoin ATM, a wire transfer, or cash handed to a stranger.
  • Asking for private numbers: a one-time code, a PIN, or a Social Security, Medicare or bank number.
  • Secrets and rush: "don't tell anyone", "don't hang up", or "this has to happen today".
  • Pretending to be someone: a grandson in jail, the IRS, Social Security, Medicare, the police, the bank, Amazon or tech support.
  • Fees to get a prize: paying first to get a prize, a grant, a refund or an inheritance.

While the call is going, Tavily also looks up the caller's number and the latest scam warnings, so the family sees why we're worried.

They're always in charge

  • Nothing is listened to until they say YES. We call them first, and they press 1 to allow it or 2 to say no.
  • They keep their own phone number and their own phone.
  • We never record or save the sound of a call. Read our privacy policy.
  • To turn it off, they call the Scam Shield number from their own phone and press 2.

What the family gets

When a call looks like a scam, we call the family right away, in their language, and they can press 1 to join the call. It works on a landline or a cell phone.

Questions

Is Scam Shield only for seniors?

It's built for older adults and the family who looks out for them, but anyone can be protected, as long as they say YES on our call.

Which scams does Scam Shield catch?

Calls that ask for gift cards, crypto, wire transfers or cash, calls that ask for codes or Social Security, Medicare or bank numbers, and callers pretending to be a grandchild, the government, a bank or tech support. AI can still miss a scam, so always call your family back on a number you already know.

Does Scam Shield work anywhere in the US?

Yes. It works with US phone numbers in every state, on a cell phone or a home landline.
