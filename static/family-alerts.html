Family scam alerts

Receive a call the moment your loved one is targeted by a scam.

Most families only learn about a scam after the money is gone. Scam Shield alerts you while the call is still in progress, so you can intervene.

How the alert works

  1. The score reaches 70. NVIDIA Nemotron scores your loved one's call from 0 to 100 in real time.
  2. Your phone rings. Scam Shield calls you in your language, English or Spanish, and explains the concern, for example: "the caller asked for gift cards."
  3. Press 1 to join the call. You're connected directly to the call, so you can tell your loved one to hang up. Press 2 to decline.
  4. Email notification. If you provided an email address, you also receive the alert by email, with a link to join the call.

Your family dashboard

  • See every protected call: the caller's number, the time, the scam score and the reasons.
  • Answer "Was this a scam?" to help improve detection.
  • Report a number to be warned when it calls, or block it entirely.
  • Pause or remove protection at any time.

Questions

How fast is the family alert?

We call you as soon as the scam score reaches 70, while the call is still in progress.

Can I join the scam call?

Yes. Press 1 during our alert call to join your loved one's call immediately.

Do family alerts come in Spanish?

Yes. Each family member chooses English or Spanish, and we call them in that language.
