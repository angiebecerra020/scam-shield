Setup in 4 steps

  1. Create your account

    Select Protect a loved one and sign up with your email address.

  2. Add the person you're protecting

    Enter their name, their 10-digit phone number, and whether it's a cell phone or a landline. Then add your phone number and email address for alerts.

  3. They give their consent

    We call them, and they press 1 to approve. Nothing is monitored without their permission.

  4. Save Scam Shield as a contact

    (570) 532-5081

    If a call seems suspicious, select Add call, choose Scam Shield, then select Merge.

To turn it off later, they call the Scam Shield number and press 2.
