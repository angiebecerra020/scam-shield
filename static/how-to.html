The 6 steps

  1. Make your account

    Tap Protect a loved one and sign up with your email.

  2. Tell us who you're protecting

    Their name, their phone with the area code, and cell or home phone. Then your phone and email for the alerts.

  3. They say yes

    We call them. They press 1 to say yes. Nothing is heard without their permission.

  4. On their phone, dial this

    **004*5705325081#

    Then press call. On Verizon, dial *715705325081 instead.

  5. Open the Scam Shield page

    Open the link from your dashboard on their phone, tap Turn on calls and allow the microphone. Keep it open. If it's closed, we call their phone instead.

  6. Save Scam Shield as a contact

    (570) 532-5081

    On a call that feels wrong? Tap Add call, pick Scam Shield, then tap Merge.

To turn it off later, dial ##004# on their phone.
