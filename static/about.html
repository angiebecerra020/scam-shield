Why we built it

Years ago, my grandmother picked up the phone. The man on the other end was polite. Patient. He was selling insurance, and he sounded like someone she could trust.

He wasn't. When the call was over, part of the retirement she had spent her whole life saving was gone.

The authorities stepped in, and we were lucky. But I'll never forget how stressful those days were for her, and for all of us. She wasn't careless. She was kind. And that's exactly what scammers count on.

Scam Shield is what I wish she'd had: something that listens with her permission, notices the tricks while the call is still happening, and brings her family in before the money leaves.

How we use NVIDIA Nemotron, Tavily and Twilio

NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory

Every new sentence of the call goes to Nemotron 3.5 Lightning through Token Factory's OpenAI compatible API. We picked the fast model on purpose: a warning only helps if it arrives during the call.

Nemotron returns a risk score from 0 to 100, a verdict, the reasons (gift cards, secrecy, urgency, a fake grandchild), who the caller claims to be, and the family's alert, written in the family's own language.

Tavily, live web search

When Nemotron says a call looks risky, Tavily runs two searches while the call is still going: has this number been reported as a scam? and what scams match what the caller just said? We build that second search from Nemotron's own reasons.

The family sees the real sources on the dashboard. Calls that look safe skip the news search, so we don't waste credits.

Twilio, the phone line

The senior says yes first, by pressing 1 on our call. Then their phone forwards calls they decline or miss to Scam Shield, or they add us to a call in 2 taps (Add call, then Merge).

Twilio's real time transcription sends us the words, and a conference room lets the family join the call with one tap.

Built to be trusted

FastAPI on Render, Supabase logins, 335 automated tests. We never record calls or save the words. A silent caller gets warned at 10 seconds and hung up at 20. Demo mode keeps a scripted call working for anyone, with no real phone.
