Landline and cell phone protection

Scam call protection for a landline or a cell phone.

Your loved one keeps their own phone and number. Their calls are forwarded to Scam Shield, which monitors for scam tactics with their consent.

On a cell phone

No app installation required. Setup takes under 5 minutes from your dashboard:

  • Calls ring on their phone as usual.
  • They open the Scam Shield page in their phone's browser and keep it open. When a number looks suspicious, they select Decline, and the call rings on that page with Scam Shield monitoring.
  • One dial code sends declined and missed calls to Scam Shield. If the page is closed, we call their phone and they press 1 to answer with Scam Shield monitoring, or 2 to answer without it.
  • The call always goes through. If they don't press anything, protection stays on.

On a home landline

Three steps at their home, using a small phone adapter (about $40):

  1. Connect the adapter to their internet router and sign in.
  2. Pick up their phone and dial a code to forward their calls to Scam Shield.
  3. Plug their phone into the adapter. Calls ring as usual.

Simple to turn off

On a cell phone, dial ##004# (on Verizon, *73). On a landline, plug the phone back into the wall jack and dial *73. Your loved one can also call the Scam Shield number and press 2.
